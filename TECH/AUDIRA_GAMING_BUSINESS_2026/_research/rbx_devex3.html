Developer Exchange (DevEx) Overview, How to Submit, Requirements

An overview of what the DevEx program is, how to submit a DevEx request, and the requirements for a user to submit a request.

DevEx Program Overview

The Roblox Developer Exchange Program (also known as DevEx) is a program that allows Roblox creators to exchange their Earned Robux for real money. While any creator can use Roblox Studio to learn to code, create experiences or items to enjoy with their friends, and even earn some Robux, only successful creators will be able to meet the high requirements (described below) to earn money through DevEx. Reaching this level of success typically requires a lot of time, effort, skill, and strategy. Even then, there is no guarantee of earning money. Regardless of goals, be it fun or career advancement, we offer creators what they need to start building for free at create.roblox.com.

 

DevEx Program Application Process for New Participants

 

How the DevEx Process Works

Once you meet the eligibility requirements, you can join the DevEx program simply by submitting a DevEx request in the Creator Hub. Submitting a DevEx request starts a review by the DevEx team where we review your eligibility for a DevEx Payout. If approved, an invitation to the DevEx Portal is submitted where you can provide your tax and payment information. Once registration is complete, a payment can be submitted. For more information, read on below:

 

DevEx Eligibility Requirements

To use DevEx, you must agree to the Developer Exchange Terms of Use and meet the requirements set out in those Terms, some of which are as follows:

  • Minimum of 30,000 earned Robux in your account
  • Have a verified email address
  • Valid DevEx portal account (an invitation is sent after review of your first submitted DevEx request)
  • 13 years of age or older
  • Community member in good standing, having complied with Roblox's Terms of Use

For the full list of DevEx eligibility requirements, please visit our Terms of Use.

When submitting a DevEx request, please only use your legal name. Nicknames, family members, friends, or joke names may result in your account’s ineligibility to participate in the DevEx program, or significantly delay your review.
