ROHANomics

The new era where play, growth, and value come together

Why RUBY is the Core

RUBY is the official in-game currency of R.O.H.A.N.2
It serves as the foundation of our entire token ecosystem.
Web2 players just play with RUBY, no wallet needed.
Web3 users can turn RUBY into tradable assets through simple steps.

Confused about which token matters? Just remember RUBY.

ROHANomics Quick Look

Sustaining economy through gameplay
Tokenomics of R.O.H.A.N.2 focus on sustaining economic circulation through gameplay
Players put in significant gameplay to convert RUBY into on-chain tokens, driving engagement and fueling the game economy.

RUBY Allocation

Allocation of RUBY
Total amount of RUBY : One billion (No additional mint)
RUBY is allocated 100% to in-game play (No team allocation)

ROHANomics Architecture

How the price of RUBY is shaped
In the Exchange, players can purchase items/CRON with RUBY, sellers get sRUBY by selling them (Selling Fee: 10%)
All RUBY spent in the game flows into the RUBY Vault, and is resold through the in-game RUBY Market.
At this point, no issuance beyond the maximum supply of One billion RUBY will ever take place.
Therefore, with a fixed total supply, the game economy stays resilient and keeps vibin.
After launch, the price of RUBY follows the DEX rate based on supply and demand.
In-game RUBY Market maintains a minimum price of $0.02.
