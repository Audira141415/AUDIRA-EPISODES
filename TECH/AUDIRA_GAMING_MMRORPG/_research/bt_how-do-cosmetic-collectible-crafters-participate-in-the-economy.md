# How Do Cosmetic Collectible Crafters Participate In The Economy?

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2FyVgFa9Zgr455X9Y2L2wN%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=6a489b69-400e-4e53-b114-ca02d83c0ad1" alt=""><figcaption><p>Big Time's Economy Value Chain.</p></figcaption></figure>

To become a [**Cosmetic Collectible**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) crafter, a player must acquire [**SPACE**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/space.md) and either an [**Armory**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/armories.md) or a [**Forge**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/forges.md).

After connecting their [**Armory**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/armories.md) or [**Forge**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/forges.md) to their [**SPACE**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/space.md)**,** the player can craft and upgrade [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) by using the required crafting materials.

Once a player finishes crafting their collectible, they can list it for sale on the [**Open Loot Marketplace**](https://openloot.com/explore).
