# Energy

Energy is our first constraint in the game. Every action you do farming will drain your energy.

![](https://198756760-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FtluPEXMYCKReQCuWUbZo%2Fuploads%2FGuZroW3Xr2zJpuVpxgQp%2FScreen%20Shot%202022-03-07%20at%206.42.25%20PM.png?alt=media\&token=034bdeb5-645a-4827-94bc-cd9be7c8de82)

## Refilling Energy

In order to increase energy, you can either wait - your energy will increase by 1% every 5 minutes.

Or you can cook some food!
