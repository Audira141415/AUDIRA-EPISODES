Rohan 2 Global: 7 Mistakes to Avoid

Posted by by George Tsagkarakis October 13, 2025 4 Min Read Blockchain Games Tags: All ROHAN 2 Rohan 2 global

Rohan 2 Global is a next-generation crypto MMORPG that combines classic fantasy RPG gameplay with blockchain integration. Players can explore vast lands, complete quests, and engage in PvP while earning real in-game rewards powered by Web3 technology. The game retains the nostalgic essence of the original Rohan series while introducing new systems for trading, gear upgrading, and tokenized progression.

If you’ve just joined Rohan 2 Global CROSS $0.1042 ▼ -1.5% or are planning to start, you’re stepping into a world full of challenges, opportunities, and grinding potential. However, many players find that early mistakes can significantly slow down their progress.

Here are seven beginner mistakes to avoid for a strong start in Rohan 2 Global:

  1. Ignoring Quests and Dungeons
    Quests and dungeons are essential for early progression, providing large amounts of XP, crucial gear, and currency rewards. Please make time to complete them daily; skipping these can hinder your leveling speed and access to essential materials.
  2. Farming Without Upgrading Gear
    Constantly farming monsters without upgrading your equipment is a major setback. Always upgrade your gear as you level up; weak weapons and armor will drastically slow down your farming efficiency and XP gain. Stronger gear leads to faster progress.
  3. Waiting for High-Level Gear Drops
    Don’t waste time hoping for rare drops from bosses or random mobs. Instead, combine two pieces of gear to create a higher rarity version. Crafting and upgrading on your own will ensure smoother progression and better farming results.
  4. Farming Without a Plan
    Rohan 2 Global rewards strategic farming rather than random grinding. Always know what you’re farming for, whether it’s codex entries, XP efficiency, or AFK survival. Identify which maps and monsters align with your goals to maximize your time and loot.
  5. Not Monitoring Your Character
    If you leave your character AFK for extended periods, be sure to check in regularly. You might find yourself dead, stuck, or disconnected, earning zero XP for hours. A quick check can save you a lot of wasted time during long grinding sessions.
  6. Joining the Wrong Guild
    Guilds in Rohan 2 Global offer powerful buffs, shared resources, and community events. Avoid joining random or inactive guilds. Play with friends or select a highly active, progressing guild steady advancement and team synergy.
  7. Misallocating Skill Points
    One of the costliest mistakes involves spending skill points randomly. Each class has unique skill trees that define its strengths. Research your class, understand which attributes matter most, and plan your skill distribution carefully. An incorrect setup can limit your combat potential later on.

Rohan 2 Global blends nostalgic MMORPG gameplay with modern crypto mechanics, allowing players to earn while they grind. However, success in this world depends on strategy, consistency, and wise choices. By avoiding these beginner mistakes, you’ll level up faster, strengthen your character, and enjoy the game to its fullest.
