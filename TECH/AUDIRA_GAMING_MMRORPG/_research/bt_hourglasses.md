# Hourglasses

**Hourglasses** are Collectibles crafted by the **Time Warden** and they serve as the main way to acquire [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md).

### **Max Issuances**

The issuance amounts in the table above are **immutable and** **will never change**.

### **Rarity**

**Hourglasses** have ten rarity tiers: **Common** through **Unique**. The higher the rarity tier, the more [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) they can generate.

## Acquiring $BIGTIME With Hourglasses

When equipped, **Hourglasses** allow players to acquire [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) while adventuring. The amount of [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) acquired increases depending on the number of equipped **Hourglasses** and their rarity.

[**$BIGTIME Token**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) generation not only depends on the Rarity but also on a player’s skill and efficiency at playing the game — the harder the content, the more [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) they will collect.

### Equipping Multiple Hourglasses

Players can spend [**Time Crystals**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/time-crystals.md) to increase the number of **Hourglasses** equipped. Players can equip up to 5 **Hourglasses** at once.

| Slot number | Time Crystal Cost |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| 1           | 0                 |
| 2           | 6,250             |
| 3           | 12,500            |
| 4           | 25,000            |
| 5           | 50,000            |

*Note: Values in the table above are subject to change for balancing reasons.*

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hourglass Equip Slot Cost**

To unlock all 5 equip slots, you will need a total of **93,750 Time Crystals** (the sum of all 4 extra equip slots), once the slots are unlocked, they will be available for all pocket watches since this is a one-time purchase.
{% endhint %}

## Hourglass Active Time

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2FAetPB1hv7fSxN2ohmKrW%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=5ab7b529-18b2-4281-8686-7156367126e7" alt=""><figcaption><p>Hourglass Cycle.</p></figcaption></figure>

An **Hourglass** has a limited **Active Time** and players will be able to generate $BIGTIME if the Hourglass is equipped and it has an **Active Time.** Once the **Hourglass** is depleted, players can recharge it via the [**Time Warden**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/time-wardens.md) by using the **Recharge Hourglass** action.
