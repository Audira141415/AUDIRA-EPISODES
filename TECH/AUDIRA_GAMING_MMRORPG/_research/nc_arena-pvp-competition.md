# Arena :: PvP Competition

## Introduction

<figure><img src="https://4131251449-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSmtywhfKXUhbjWG1su6V%2Fuploads%2FPZ3kWxZeUrL577Z9JhxN%2FIMG_4661.PNG?alt=media&amp;token=edb2ec51-4765-44d1-b7a5-4007c46f2478" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Arena is a competitive feature of the decentralized role-playing game, Nine Chronicles. It allows players to challenge each other in battles to earn in-game rewards such as NCG (Nine Chronicles Gold) and exclusive NFTs.&#x20;

The arena seasons provide a structured format where players can compete weekly to climb the rankings and earn a share of a substantial reward pool. This arena setup not only enhances the game's competitive aspect but also provides a play-to-earn opportunity, rewarding active and skilled players.

## Fun Elements for Players

<figure><img src="https://4131251449-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSmtywhfKXUhbjWG1su6V%2Fuploads%2FLA8x1axXqvLB4cv0Orm3%2FIMG_4666.PNG?alt=media&amp;token=a7aee8ea-5b07-4037-90f8-79737e8ec684" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Nine Chronicles Arena offers players various enjoyable elements, including strategic PvP battles, staking opportunities, and the chance to earn exclusive seasonal rewards. Players are motivated by the thrill of climbing the leaderboard and the prospect of gaining rare NFTs and substantial NCG rewards.&#x20;

The arena fosters a competitive environment where both skill and strategy are crucial, and the continuous updates and varied challenges keep the gameplay fresh and engaging.

At the end of the season, the final Arena rankings can be viewed on the Nine Chronicles portal: <https://nine-chronicles.com/arena-rank>

## Season Composition and Rewards by Type

<figure><img src="https://4131251449-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSmtywhfKXUhbjWG1su6V%2Fuploads%2FhrsndPOqaYocoH3JJOSw%2FIMG_4660.PNG?alt=media&amp;token=f32192e7-f5e3-4700-a97d-ce2f78342a33" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Arena seasons typically last for about a month, encompassing several weeks of competitions. Each season is divided into weekly segments where players can earn rewards at the end of each week. For example, Arena Season 0 had a total reward pool of 896K NCG and 240 season-exclusive NFTs, with weekly NCG rewards distributed to the top-ranking players.&#x20;

Moreover, end-of-season rewards included prestigious titles and mythical-grade costumes. The rewards are designed to incentivize both participation and high performance, ensuring that all players have opportunities to benefit from their efforts.

## Reward Distribution Logic and Contribution to NCG Ecosystem

The reward distribution in Nine Chronicles Arena is based on a combination of player rankings and accrued medals, which reflect their participation and performance. At the end of each season, the NCG reward pool is divided among participants according to their share of medals. This system ensures that even less experienced or lower-ranked players receive some rewards, promoting broad engagement.&#x20;

Additionally, the staking mechanism allows players to lock up NCG to earn extra rewards, thus reducing the circulating supply and potentially increasing the value of NCG. This structure not only rewards player activity but also helps maintain a balanced and sustainable in-game economy.
