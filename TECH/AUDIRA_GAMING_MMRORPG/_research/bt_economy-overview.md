## Our Cosmetic Based Economy

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2FelWzBBgyv5bab8D6dAlY%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=d9cecb79-a802-4a69-b6ad-e711623710bc" alt=""><figcaption><p>Free-to-Play cosmetic based economy compared to Big Time's Economy Structure.</p></figcaption></figure>

At Big Time, one of our main design pillars is fair play, which is why we built our economy to be a “Cosmetic Based Economy”\
\
For comparison, in a typical free-to-play cosmetic economy, something such as League of Legends, Fortnite, or Valorant, the game studio would sell skins directly to the players.

At Big Time, instead, we empower our players by giving them the ability to craft cosmetics and letting them control the creation and selling of cosmetics in the game. By enabling them to do that,  we are inviting them to be deeply rooted in our business model.

The way it works is that we create the 3D models, and then list the recipes for players to craft.

## Our Special Cosmetic Collectibles

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2FY3PoUNlaKHPsYkuJ5JCx%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=c32bcaf9-2f3e-4676-a8cc-e51bdc1113c1" alt=""><figcaption><p>Special perks about our Cosmetic Collectibles.</p></figcaption></figure>

Our Cosmetics look awesome and they convey status and uniqueness. Our game is a multiplayer Action-RPG which makes it an excellent canvas to show off your unique Possessions.

All our Cosmetics are **Digital Collectibles** with a limited and immutable supply, meaning any given line of cosmetics will have a fixed number of copies, and that number will never change.

Our [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) will also be player-owned, meaning that players will always have the ability to trade or sell them whether on [**Open Loot**](https://wiki.bigtime.gg/big-time-economy/www.openloot.com) or outside our platforms.

Lastly, Owning [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) would give players access to exclusive areas in the game, which could take the form of an exclusive lounge or a portal that has different non-power-related loot tables.

## Crafting Collectibles

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2F3XEzfXXuxPfVNpKYIXmk%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=961e9986-4d63-4c6f-a1e7-6d8345c0af8b" alt=""><figcaption><p>SPACE, Utility NFTs and Crafting.</p></figcaption></figure>

Players can participate in the crafting economy by acquiring [**SPACE**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/space.md) and a [**Utility Collectible**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops.md) such as an [**Armory**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/armories.md), [**Forge**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/forges.md), or [**Time Warden**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/time-wardens.md).

[**SPACE**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/space.md) is Big Time’s version of digital land, and [**Utility Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops.md) are the factories that enable the crafting of  [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) and [**Hourglasses**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/hourglasses.md).

## $BIGTIME Token Design

[**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) is a fair launch token with no allocation to the team or investors. The primary way to get [**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) will be through in-game drops.

[**Hourglasses**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/hourglasses.md) are **limited supply** artifacts that, once equipped, would allow the player to start generating [**$BIGTIME Tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) while playing the game.

<figure><img src="https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/ma0O7-Y3j4AUpGnxfqP6rw_8CP6Ysf0GRlQjsp8S9fXk2k5JivPwYdFFMoQ4pon4qg-UpWIFTxvX5dBCMM1pvUIb0RETXvAT1leatuS2CEVzZS7XhbIuNtAgNpGqXM-bbiClqEDnPKkEXB9ymyy1Subt0Q=s2048" alt=""><figcaption><p>Supply &#x26; Demand Dynamics of $BIGTIME Token depending on our player base.</p></figcaption></figure>

[**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) is a cryptocurrency that acts as the glue of our economy. Since [**Hourglasses**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/hourglasses.md) are limited in supply, only a **fixed number** of players can equip them at any given moment, regardless of the number of active players.

On the other hand, [**$BIGTIME tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) can be spent by any player, meaning the potential sinks for [**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) in the economy are **limited only by the number of players in our game**.

The dynamic that emerges from these two systems is that if the **player base grows**, the supply of [**$BIGTIME tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) **is capped** and won’t ever change, but the **sinks of** [**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) **will keep growing** as the player base grows.

## The Value Chain of Big Time's Economy

To summarize it all, Big Time's Economy Value Chain in Action looks like this:

<figure><img src="https://3228690745-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FmbENrMdsRg8sJLWPWxS9%2Fuploads%2FGLRdOnMfIKAm74RWM7iH%2FValue_Chain_In_Action.png?alt=media&amp;token=d097a2b0-86bf-4d02-8e44-e1ad06ce026a" alt=""><figcaption><p>Big Time's Value Chain In Action.</p></figcaption></figure>

Now to bring it all together, our value chain would look something like this:

1. [**Time wardens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/time-wardens.md) would craft [**Hourglasses**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/hourglasses.md).
2. [**Hourglasses**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/hourglasses.md) are required by our [**$BIGTIME**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) suppliers who would equip them and start generating [**$BIGTIME tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md).
3. [**$BIGTIME tokens**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/resources/usdbigtime-tokens.md) are required by our [**Armory**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/armories.md) and [**Forge**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops/forges.md) owners, who need them to craft [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md).
4. [**Cosmetic Collectibles**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/cosmetic-collectibles.md) then would be listed on our marketplace for all our non-[**Utility Collectible**](/big-time-economy/economy-components/personal-metaverse/workshops.md) owners to buy.

In a nutshell, that's how our game economy would work.
