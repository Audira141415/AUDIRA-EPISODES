The badminton mixed doubles tournament at the [[2016 Summer Olympics]] took place from 11 to 17 August at Riocentro - Pavilion 4. The seeding was decided on 21 July 2016.<ref name=":0"/>

[[Indonesia at the 2016 Summer Olympics|Indonesia]]'s [[Tontowi Ahmad]] and [[Liliyana Natsir]] defeated [[Malaysia at the 2016 Summer Olympics|Malaysia]]'s [[Chan Peng Soon]] and [[Goh Liu Ying]] in the final, 21–14, 21–12 to win the gold medal in mixed doubles badminton at the [[2016 Summer Olympics]].

Natsir was the second Indonesian woman Olympic gold medalist, after [[Susi Susanti]] in [[Badminton at the 1992 Summer Olympics|1992]], and was later succeeded by [[Greysia Polii]] and [[Apriyani Rahayu]] in [[Badminton at the 2020 Summer Olympics|2021]].
Natsir competed in the [[Badminton at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Mixed doubles|mixed doubles at the 2012 Summer Olympics]] with partner [[Tontowi Ahmad]] as fourth seed. The duo topped the group C standings without dropping a game. In the quarter-finals, they beat German pair [[Michael Fuchs (badminton)|Michael Fuchs]] and [[Birgit Michels]], but they had to admit the toughness of the second seeds [[Xu Chen]] and [[Ma Jin]] in the semi-finals. They finally could not present a medal for Indonesia after losing the bronze medal match to [[Joachim Fischer Nielsen]] and [[Christinna Pedersen]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Indonesian badminton athletes get no single medal in London |url=https://en.antaranews.com/news/83760/indonesian-badminton-athletes-get-no-single-medal-in-london |publisher=[[Antara (news agency)|Antara]] |date=4 June 2018}}</ref>
Natsir won the [[All England Open Badminton Championships|All England Open]] for three consecutive times from 2012 to 2014. Natsir participated at the [[Badminton at the 2014 Asian Games|2014 Asian Games]] in Incheon, South Korea, and won a silver medal in [[Badminton at the 2014 Asian Games – Mixed doubles|mixed doubles]] with Ahmad.
