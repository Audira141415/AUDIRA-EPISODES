Published 17 August 2016 Share close panel Share page Copy link About sharing Image source, Reuters Image caption, Tontowi Ahmad (left) and Liliyana Natsir - Natsir won a mixed-doubles silver at Beijing 2008 Liliyana Natsir and Tontowi Ahmad of Indonesia beat Malaysia's Goh Liu Ying and Chan Peng Soon to claim gold in badminton's mixed doubles. The Indonesians were always comfortably ahead, winning 21-4 21-12. Natsir had won silver in the same event at Beijing in 2008, with this victory seeing Indonesia claim its seventh Olympic gold, all in badminton. China's Zhang Nan and Zhao Yunlei, gold medallists at London 2012, took the bronze.
