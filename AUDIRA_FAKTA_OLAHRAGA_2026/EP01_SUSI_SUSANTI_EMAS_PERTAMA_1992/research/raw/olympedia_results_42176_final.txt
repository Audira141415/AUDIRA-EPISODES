Did you know?
 The 1904 Olympics were originally set to be held in Chicago. St. Louis, which hosted the Louisiana Purchase Exposition, wanted to incorporate the Olympics in the Expo’s program, even threatening to stage rival events if Chicago did not yield the Games.
