# Source Register — EP07 Ibnu Battuta: 29 Tahun di Jalan

Date read for all rows: 2026-11-12. Raw text is in `research/raw/` (file named in the last column).
Levels: PRIMARY (primary historical text, official body, peer-reviewed paper), CLASSIC (classical historians and medieval texts), SECONDARY (encyclopedias, reputable books).
The Rihla is a 14th-century primary text dictated by Ibn Battuta. It is read here in English translation (Gibb 1929 selections; Gibb & Beckingham 1994 full translation), so its rows are marked CLASSIC (translation).
"via" = the source was read through a quotation or citation in another work; the original was not opened.
