The '''International Year of Light and Light-based Technologies 2015''' or '''International Year of Light 2015''' ('''IYL 2015''') was a United Nations observance that aimed to raise awareness of the achievements of light science and its applications, and its importance to humankind. IYL 2015 opening ceremonies was held on 19–20 January 2015 in Paris.

==History==
The International Year of Light is "a global initiative adopted by the United Nations to raise awareness of how optical technologies promote sustainable development and provide solutions to worldwide challenges in energy, education, agriculture, communications and health."<ref name=IYL-FAQ-About>{{cite web |title=International Year of Light FAQ |url=http://light2015.org/Home/About/FAQ.html |website=International Year of Light |access-date=7 December 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141205104459/http://www.light2015.org/Home/About/FAQ.html |archive-date=December 5, 2014 }}</ref>

UNESCO delegates from Ghana and Mexico introduced the proposal to the executive board, explaining the motivation and mission underlying the International Year of Light. The resolution was adopted by the executive board joined by co-signatories from a further 28 Board Members.<ref>{{cite web|title=UNESCO Executive Board supports International Year of Light in 2015|url=http://c.ymcdn.com/sites/www.eps.org/resource/resmgr/events/IYOL_PressRelease.pdf|website=[[UNESCO]]|access-date=10 December 2014|date=23 October 2012}}</ref> The resolution proposing an IYL 2015 was placed before the 190th Session of the [[UNESCO]] executive board held in Paris from 13–18 October 2012 by Ghana, Mexico, and the Russian Federation (Board Members) and New Zealand (UNESCO Member State).<ref name="UN-190EX/Decisions-2012">{{cite web|title=Decisions Adopted by the Executive Board at Its 190th Session|url=http://unesdoc.unesco.org/images/0021/002181/218189e.pdf|website=[[United Nations]]|access-date=10 December 2014|location=Paris, France|date=18 November 2012}}</ref>

The resolution A/RES/68/221 proclaiming the IYL 2015 was adopted on 20 December 2013 during a plenary meeting of the 68th Session of the [[United Nations General Assembly|UN General Assembly]], with the General Assembly acting on the recommendation of its Second Committee ([[United Nations General Assembly Second Committee|Economic and Financial]]) during consideration of an Agenda item on Science and Technology for Development.<ref name=UN-71stMtg-2013>{{cite web|title=General Assembly, Taking up Second Committee Reports, Adopts 41 Resolutions, 2 Decisions, Deferring Action on Small Islands Text |url=http://www.un.org/press/en//2013/ga11478.doc.htm|website=[[United Nations]]|publisher=United Nations - General Assembly|access-date=10 December 2014}}</ref>

[[Prince Andrew, Duke of York]] is a patron of IYL 2015.<ref name=DukeOfYork-2014>{{cite web|title=The Duke of York » Supporting The Queen » Patronages » Science, Technology and Engineering|url=http://thedukeofyork.org/supporting-the-queen/patronages/science-technology-and-engineering/|website=[[The Duke of York]]|access-date=10 December 2014}}</ref>

===Organization===
The IYL 2015 has been endorsed by a number of international Scientific Unions and the [[International Council for Science]]. The IYL 2015 will be administered by an International steering committee convened by [[John Michael Dudley|John M. Dudley]] in collaboration with the UNESCO International Basic Sciences Programme and a Secretariat at The Abdus Salam [[International Centre for Theoretical Physics]] which is a UNESCO Category 1 Institute.

==Activities==

===Opening ceremony===
The International Year of Light was officially launched on 19 January 2015 with a two-day opening ceremony at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris. The opening ceremony introduced the key themes of the year, acting as inspiration for events worldwide during 2015. Over a thousand participants attended the event. Speakers and attendees included international diplomats and decision-makers, Nobel laureates, CEOs, and science and industry leaders from across the globe. The event also featured works from light artists around the world including Finnish light artist Kari Kola and US design team Light At Play.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.light2015.org/Home/About/Latest-News/December2014/Laun-Opening-Ceremony.html | title=The International Year of Light and Light-based Technologies 2015 Opening Ceremony | work=light2015.org | date=12 December 2015 | access-date=10 June 2016 | archive-date=3 June 2016 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160603141904/http://www.light2015.org/Home/About/Latest-News/December2014/Laun-Opening-Ceremony.html | url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web| url=http://projects.registerguard.com/rg/news/local/32166698-75/opportunities-glow-bright-for-eugene-developers-of-light-art.html.csp |title=Globe-trotting orb |work= The Register-Guard |date= 19 September 2014}}</ref>

===US events===
[[File:The Radiance Orb at the National Academy of Sciences.jpg|thumb|Musicians are bathed in LED light from the Radiance Orb, which responds to their playing before the start of the Light for a Better World celebration in Washington D.C. Credit: Jason Socrates Bardi.]]

The steering committee organized two flagship events in the United States: '''Wonders of Light: Family Science Fun''' held at the [[Smithsonian Institution|Smithsonian's]] [[National Museum of the American Indian]] and '''Light for a Better World – A Celebration of U.S. Innovation''' held at the [[National Academy of Sciences]].<ref>{{cite web| url=http://nsfdirectorfieldnotes.tumblr.com/post/129574669426/international-year-of-light |title=International Year of Light |work= Field Notes from the NSF Director |date= 21 September 2015}}</ref>

The '''Wonders of Light''' event was sponsored by the [[National Science Foundation]] and was attended by hundreds of children and their parents. Exhibits included a variety of hands-on-optics and photonics displays.<ref>{{cite web| url=https://light2015blog.org/2015/09/25/huge-success-for-wonders-of-light-a-cornerstone-u-s-iyl-2015-event/ |title=Huge Success for "Wonders of Light," a Cornerstone U.S. IYL 2015 Event |work= IYL 2015 Blog |date= 25 September 2015}}</ref>

The '''Light for a Better World''' symposium was sponsored by numerous organizations including the National Science Foundation, National Academy of Sciences, [[The Optical Society]], [[American Institute of Physics]], [[American Physical Society]], [[IEEE Photonics Society]] and [[SPIE]]. Speakers at the event included: 2014 Nobel laureate [[Shuji Nakamura]], National Science Foundation director [[France Córdova]], Michael Liehr (SUNY Polytechnic), Gerald Duffy (General Electric) and 2014 Nobel laureate [[Eric Betzig]].<ref>{{cite web| url=https://light2015blog.org/2015/09/17/light-for-a-better-world-a-celebration-of-u-s-innovation-at-the-national-academy-of-sciences/ |title=Light for a Better World – A Celebration of U.S. Innovation |work= IYL 2015 Blog |date= 17 September 2015}}</ref>

===Ibn al-Haytham campaign===
[[File:Book of Optics Cover Page.jpg|thumb|Ibn al-Haytham's Book of Optics cover page]]
The IYL 2015 launched<ref>{{cite journal|title=Videos: Paris speakers pay tribute to light|journal=SPIE Newsroom|date=2015 |doi=10.1117/3.201502.01|url=http://spie.org/x112471.xml |last1=Spie |url-access=subscription}}</ref> at the [[UNESCO]] headquarters in [[Paris]] on 19 January 2015, with the unveiling of [[1001 Inventions and the World of Ibn Al-Haytham]] which is a global campaign that includes a series of interactive exhibits, workshops and live shows about [[Alhazen|Ibn al-Haytham]]'s achievements in optics, mathematics and astronomy, and his importance in laying the foundations of the present day [[Scientific method|scientific experimental method]]. The campaign was created by the [[1001 Inventions]] organisation, which is a founding partner of the International Year of Light.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.light2015.org/Home/About/Latest-News/November2014/Ibn-Al-Haytham-to-be-the-focus-of-the-International-Year-of-Light-through-partnering-with-1001-Inventions-.html |title=Ibn Al-Haytham to be a focus of the International Year of Light through partnering with 1001 Inventions |publisher=UNESCO |access-date=4 February 2015 |archive-date=26 September 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180926090150/http://www.light2015.org/Home/About/Latest-News/November2014/Ibn-Al-Haytham-to-be-the-focus-of-the-International-Year-of-Light-through-partnering-with-1001-Inventions-.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

As part of the international campaign, UNESCO will also host an exhibition and a conference, to take place on 14 September 2015, titled '''The Islamic Golden Age of Science for the Knowledge-Based Society'''.<ref name=UNESCO-1000-2014>{{cite web| url=http://www.unesco.org/new/en/media-services/single-view/news/1000_years_of_arabic_optics_to_be_a_focus_of_the_international_year_of_light_in_2015 |title=1000 Years of Arabic Optics to be a Focus of the International Year of Light in 2015 |work=[[United Nations]] |access-date=27 November 2014}}</ref> In line with the event three organizations from Hyderabad viz MS Academy, Mesco and ILM Foundation launched i Quiz 2015 – a National level quiz competition on ‘Golden Age of Science’. The competition will be held across 64 cities in India and the grand finale will be held in Hyderabad<ref>{{Cite web | title=School Registration {{!}} | url=http://ibnalhaytham.in/school-registration | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402114403/http://ibnalhaytham.in/school-registration | access-date=2026-01-14 | archive-date=2015-04-02}}</ref>

===Scientific anniversaries===
The major scientific anniversaries that will be celebrated during IYL 2015 are:
* 1015: [[Book of Optics|Works on optics]] by [[Alhazen|Ibn Al-Haytham]]
* 1815: The notion of light as a [[wave]] proposed by [[Augustin-Jean Fresnel|Fresnel]]
* 1865: [[A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field|The electromagnetic theory of light propagation]] proposed by [[James Clerk Maxwell|Maxwell]]
* 1915: [[Albert Einstein|Einstein]]'s theory of the [[photoelectric effect]] in 1905 and of the embedding of light in cosmology through [[general relativity]]
* 1965: Discovery of the [[cosmic microwave background]] by [[Arno Penzias|Penzias]] and [[Robert Woodrow Wilson|Wilson]], and [[Charles K. Kao|Charles Kao]]'s achievements concerning the transmission of light in fibers for optical communication

=== International conference: Colombia in the International Year of Light 2015 ===
The International conference ''[http://indico.cern.ch/event/357191/ Colombia in the International Year of Light 2015]'' will be hosted by three universities of Colombia in 16–19 June 2015. The conference will allow for discussions about the state-of-the-art in this field, in the company of world-renowned scientists (See the [http://indico.cern.ch/event/357191/program scientific programme] and the [http://indico.cern.ch/event/357191/material/paper/ conference announcements]). Organizers of this conference include: The ''Universidad de los Andes'' ([http://www.uniandes.edu.co/component/content/article/656-about-uniandes University of the Andes] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150628230222/http://www.uniandes.edu.co/component/content/article/656-about-uniandes |date=28 June 2015 }}), Bogotá, the ''Universidad Nacional de Colombia'' ([http://unal.edu.co/en/ National University of Colombia]), Bogotá and Medellín, the ''Universidad de Antioquia'' ([http://portal.udea.edu.co/wps/portal/udea/web/home University of Antioquia]), Medellín, the ''[http://www.accefyn.org.co/sp/about_us.htm Academia Colombiana de Ciencias Exactas, Físicas y Naturales] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150317071518/http://www.accefyn.org.co/sp/about_us.htm |date=17 March 2015 }}'' (Colombian Academy of Exact, Physical and Natural Sciences), Colombian research groups working on topics related to optical sciences, and academic programs at the undergraduate and graduate levels.{{fact|date=August 2023}}
