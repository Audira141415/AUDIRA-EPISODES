SOURCE: Saudi Press Agency, "Grand Mosque Expands Zamzam Water Distribution, Daily Quality Testing for Hajj Season", Makkah, 21 May 2026. https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2594029 (read 2026-11-10 via browser)

... upgrading the Grand Mosque's Zamzam water distribution ecosystem to accommodate the rising number of pilgrims during the 1447 AH Hajj season ...
The infrastructure includes 169 drinking fountains, roughly 14,892 daily dispensers, and the distribution of over 2,146,000 cups each day. Daily consumption averages 1,622 cubic meters of Zamzam water, backed by 70 daily safety and quality tests.
