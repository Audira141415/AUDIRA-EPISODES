'''Jabal Thawr''' ({{langx|ar|جَبَل ثَوْر}}) is a mountain in [[Saudi Arabia]], located in the lower part of [[Mecca]] to the south of the district of [[Misfalah]].<ref>[http://www.isesco.org.ma/English/Culture/Mekka/P5.htm Celebrating Makkah al-Mukarramah as a capital of Islamic culture for 2005<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> The height of the mountain is {{convert|750|m|ft|abbr=on}}.{{citation needed|date=March 2023}}

==Cave==
The mountain is notable for housing a [[cave]] known as ''Ghār Thawr'' ({{langx|ar|غَار ثَوْر|link=no}}), in which the Islamic prophet [[Muhammad]] took refuge from the [[Quraysh]], during the [[Hegira|migration to Medina]]. For most Muslims, the cave is of religious significance, and is thus visited by many pilgrims and tourists. Mount Thawr is located {{convert|5|mile|km|abbr=off}} away from Mecca. In Islam, it is believed that Muhammad stayed here as a refugee with his companion [[Abu Bakr]].
