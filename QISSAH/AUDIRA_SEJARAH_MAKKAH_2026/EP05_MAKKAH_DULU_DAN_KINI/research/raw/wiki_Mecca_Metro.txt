The '''Mecca Metro''' or '''Makkah Metro''' is a [[Rapid transit|metro]] system with four planned lines in the city of [[Mecca]], [[Saudi Arabia]]. The Metro was constructed by [[China Railway Construction Corporation]]<ref>{{cite magazine|title=As China Woos Overseas Business, State-Owned Firms Take a Hit|url=http://content.time.com/time/world/article/0,8599,2033238,00.html|access-date=2017-06-11|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=2010-11-28}}</ref> and is run by Mecca Mass Rail Transit Company (MMRTC). The metro forms part of the 62-billion-riyal Mecca Public Transport Programme (MPTP), which will include integrated bus services.<ref name=prequal /><ref name=consultancy>{{cite journal |url=http://www.railwaygazette.com/news/urban/single-view/view/prasarana-wins-makkah-metro-consultancy-contract.html |title= Prasarana wins Makkah metro consultancy contract |journal=[[Railway Gazette International]] |date=2014-11-28}}</ref><ref name=approved>{{cite journal |url=http://www.railwaygazette.com/news/single-view/view/jeddah-and-makkah-metro-plans-approved.html |title=Jeddah and Makkah metro plans approved |journal=Railway Gazette International |date=2012-08-17}}</ref>

The four proposed metro lines<ref name="approved" /> will be in addition to the existing [[Al Mashaaer Al Mugaddassah Metro line|Al Mashaer Al Mugaddassah Metro line]]: 18.1&nbsp;km, connecting [[Mecca]], Arafat, Muzdalifa, and Mina opened in November 2010.<ref name=opening>{{cite journal |url=http://www.railwaygazette.com/nc/news/single-view/view/hajj-pilgrims-take-the-metro-to-makkah.html |title=Hajj pilgrims take the metro to Makkah |journal=Railway Gazette International |date=2010-11-15}}</ref><ref name="systra.com">{{cite news |url=http://www.systra.com/IMG/pdf/fiche_metro_la_mecque_angl.pdf |title=Mecca Metro |newspaper=[[SYSTRA]] |access-date=2015-09-28 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315035412/https://www.systra.com/IMG/pdf/fiche_metro_la_mecque_angl.pdf |url-status=dead}}</ref>

Many people utilize the Mecca Metro during the [[Hajj]].

==Planning==
In August 2012, it was announced that the [[Saudi government]] had approved a US$16.5 billion budget to build  four metro lines ({{convert|182|km|mi|sp=us}}) of the system.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.bloomberg.com/news/2012-08-14/saudi-cabinet-approves-16-5-billion-mecca-metro-system.html |title=Saudi Cabinet Approves $16.5 Billion Mecca Metro System |work=[[Bloomberg News]] |date=2012-08-14 |access-date=2014-06-07}}</ref> The announcement gave an estimated time period to completion of 10 years. Invitations for tenders were due to be issued in January 2013.<ref name=tenders>{{cite news |url=http://www.saudigazette.com.sa/index.cfm?method=home.regcon&contentid=20121025140747 |title=Makkah Metro tender to be issued in January |work=[[Saudi Gazette]] |date=2012-10-25 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140407081119/http://www.saudigazette.com.sa/index.cfm?method=home.regcon&contentid=20121025140747 |archive-date=2014-04-07 }}</ref>

Four new lines are to be built.<ref>{{cite journal |url=http://www.railwaygazette.com/news/projects-infrastructure/single-view/view/makkah-metro-expansion-plan.html |title=Makkah metro expansion plan |journal=Railway Gazette International |date=2011-08-11}}</ref>

*'''Line {{rint|mecca|A}}''' : will connect [[Mecca]] to large multilevel parking facilities from the south to the northeast.
*'''Line {{rint|mecca|B}}''' : is a straight link between Mina and Mecca and continues parallel to the [[Haramain High Speed Railway|HHSR]] into the southwest.
*'''Line {{rint|mecca|C}}''' : will connect Arafat and Mina to the west side of Mecca and continues to the northwest.
*'''Line {{rint|mecca|D}}''' : will connect the south to Mecca with a straight extension to the west.<ref name="MEED" />

Work on the {{convert|188|km|mi|adj=on|sp=us}} long metro network with 87 new stations had been expected to commence construction in 2015,<ref name=prequal>{{cite journal |url=http://www.raillynews.com/2013/al-mashaaer-al-mugaddassah-metro-project-macca-saudi-arabia-video/ |title=Al Mashaaer Al Mugaddassah Metro Project, Macca Saudi Arabia |website=raillynews.com |date=2013-10-25 |access-date=2015-06-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150714230450/http://www.raillynews.com/2013/al-mashaaer-al-mugaddassah-metro-project-macca-saudi-arabia-video/ |archive-date=2015-07-14 |url-status=dead }}</ref> then in 2016,<ref name=GN.com>{{cite news |url=http://gulfnews.com/news/gulf/uae/makkah-to-have-four-metro-lines-88-stations-1.1326029 |title=Makkah to have four metro lines, 88 stations - Phase one of 188-km metro network to begin work in 2016 |newspaper=[[Gulf News]] |date=2014-04-29 |access-date=2014-06-07}}</ref> but had not started ever since. After a failed tender in 2017,<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.constructionweekonline.com/article-45132-official-tender-for-makkah-metro-may-be-awarded-in-2018/|title=Official: Tender for Makkah Metro may be awarded in 2018|last=Abdel-Razzaq|first=Jumana|website=www.constructionweekonline.com|date=28 June 2017 |access-date=2018-03-10}}</ref> the Saudi government restarted the project in summer 2024 by ordering a feasibility study on phase 1 of the project. This phase with a US$8 billion budget covers planned line B and most parts of line C, while line A will be in the second phase, and line D plus a northern extension of line C will be part of phase 3.<ref name="MEED">{{Cite web |url=https://www.meed.com/saudi-arabia-restarts-mecca-metro-project |title=Saudi Arabia restarts Mecca metro project |author=Yasir Iqbal |work=MEED |date=2024-06-10 |access-date=2024-07-11}}</ref>

MMRTC has appointed [[Prasarana Malaysia]] to provide consultancy services during Phase 1, which covers the construction of two metro lines, totaling 45.1&nbsp;km and 22 stations by 2019, But never came into frution.<ref name=consultancy />
