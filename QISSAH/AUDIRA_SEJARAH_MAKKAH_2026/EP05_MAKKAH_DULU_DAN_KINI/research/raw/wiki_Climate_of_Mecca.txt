[[Mecca]] features a [[hot desert climate]]. Mecca retains its hot temperature in winter, which can range from {{convert|18|C|F}} at night to {{convert|30|C|F}} in the day. Summer temperatures are extremely hot, often being over {{convert|40|C|F}} during the day, dropping to {{convert|30|C|F}} at night.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://thebahrainpropertysite.com/mecca-weather.html MAKKAH WEATHER AND TEMPERATURE actual five ten fifteen day ecast<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>  Rain usually falls in Makkah in small amounts between November and January. The [[rain]]fall, as scant as it is, also presents the threat of flooding and has been a danger since the earliest times. According to Al-Kurdī, there had been 89 historic [[flood]]s by 1965, including several in the period. In the last century the most severe one occurred in 1941. Since then, [[dam]]s have been constructed to ameliorate the problem.

==Factors==
Mecca is at an elevation of 277&nbsp;m above sea level, and approximately 80&nbsp;km inland from the Red Sea.<ref name=autogenerated1 /> Flash floods are common during winter season even though the amount of precipitation is low. Since Mecca is located in a desert, dust storms are common in the city.

*'''Shamal winds''' occur in Mecca mostly during summer months, these Shamal winds are very dusty and remain at peak in the morning but decreases at night. These winds also occur in winter but not often.<ref>[https://books.google.com/books?id=2cmmgwUJH98C&pg=PA31&vq=shamal&dq=The+Gulf+War+and+the+Environment&source=gbs_search_s&cad=0 The Gulf War and the environment – Google Books<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
*'''Sandstorms''' and '''Dust storms''' both affect Mecca almost every month but especially during summer months.<ref>[http://www.eosnap.com/?s=dust+storm+n+peninsula+march Earth Snapshot • dust storm n peninsula march<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
*'''Westerly winds''' bring thunderstorms to Mecca during winter and [[hail]]storms sometimes also occur.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.khaleejtimes.com/DisplayArticle09.asp?xfile=%2Fmiddleeast%2F2010%2FNovember%2Fmiddleeast_November367.xml&section=middleeast |title=Pilgrims rush as hail storm hits Mina valley<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=2019-11-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150416011110/http://www.khaleejtimes.com/DisplayArticle09.asp?xfile=%2Fmiddleeast%2F2010%2FNovember%2Fmiddleeast_November367.xml&section=middleeast |archive-date=2015-04-16 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Extreme weather events==
Since [[Mecca]] is located in a low-lying region, it is threatened by seasonal flash floods despite the low amount of annual precipitation. There are less than {{convert|110|mm|in}} of rainfall during the year, mainly falling in the winter months. Temperatures are high throughout the year and in summer it may reach {{convert|45|C|F}}.<ref>[http://www.britannica.com/EBchecked/topic/371782/Mecca/37822/Climate Mecca ( ) :: Climate - Britannica Online Encyclopedia<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> The following are [[extreme weather]] events in Mecca and the surrounding area.

*In November 2009, [[Makkah Province]] was badly affected when [[2009 Jeddah floods|record-breaking rainfall]] of {{convert|90|mm|in}} hit the province causing flash floods all over the province. It was the worst flood in 27 years.<ref>[http://www.gazette.com.sa/index.cfm?method=home.regcon&contentID=2009112655554  Gazette - Flooding kills 12 in Jeddah, Thousands of pilgrims stranded on highway<!-- Bot generated title -->]{{Dead link|date=November 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
*In November 2010, a thunderstorm killed 3 people in Makkah city following heavy rainfall. Most of the city remained under flood warning  the entire night.<ref>[http://www.nst.com.my/nst/articles/ThunderstormkillsthreeinMecca/Article/ Thunderstorm kills three in Mecca<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
*In December 2010, once again the city was flooded when light to moderate intensity rainfall battered the holy city, claiming the lives of 4 people. While condition in [[Makkah Province]] was also comparable to the provincial capital.<ref>http://arabnews.com//article226865.ece{{Dead link|date=July 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref><ref>http://arabnews.com//article226856.ece{{Dead link|date=July 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
*In January 2011, heavy rainfall created flood like situation in the provinces especially Jeddah where {{convert|111|mm|in}} occurred in just 3 hours killing four people.<ref>[http://photoblog.msnbc.msn.com/_news/2011/01/27/5934677-heavy-rains-cause-floods-in-jeddah-- MSNBC]{{dead link|date=August 2024|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref><ref>http://arabnews.com// {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121012000127/http://arabnews.com/ |date=2012-10-12 }}
