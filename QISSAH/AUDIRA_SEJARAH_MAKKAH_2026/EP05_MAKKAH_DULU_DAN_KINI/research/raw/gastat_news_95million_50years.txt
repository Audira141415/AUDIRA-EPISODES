General Authority for Statistics: Over 95 million Hajj pilgrims hosted and served by KSA over the last 50 years
From 1390 H until last year 1439 H General Authority for Statistics: Over 95 million Hajj pilgrims hosted and served by KSA over the last 50 years The task of counting the numbers of pilgrims every year is one of the most important tasks assigned to the General Authority for Statistics (GAS). The task was entrusted to GAS in 1390 H back when it still operated under the designation of Department of Statistics and Information. This aimed at providing accurate information to assist all government entities in the planning and development of everything related to the service of the guests of Allah. The task continued throughout these years, witnessing year after year developments in counting and inventory methods and statistical outputs including data and indicators. The Authority is tasked with completing three statistical projects during every Hajj season (inventory of the numbers of pilgrims, statistics of services offered to pilgrims, and Hajj work management indicators) with the goal of providing the largest amount of data needed to support decision- and policy-makers in achieving the highest levels of accuracy and comprehensiveness. The Authority indicated that the total number of pilgrims served by the Kingdom during the last 50 years amounted to 95,853,017 pilgrims, noting that the numbers exceeded 2 millions in 19 Hajj seasons, while the numbers of pilgrims during the last 25 years reached 54,465,253 pilgrims. Moreover, the numbers of pilgrims during the last 10 years reached 23,796,977 pilgrims, as per the GAS official statistics. The hajj statistics program is aimed at complementing all other future plans and programs in order to provide necessary services for the Hajj pilgrim, including social, health, security, food, and transport services.This is done by producing timed and accurate data on the numbers of pilgrims and estimated size of workforce needed to serve pilgrims and maintain their security and comfort during the Hajj season every year. The pilgrims data and numbers are also used by entities entrusted with studying basic installations and facilities in Mecca and holy sites and are taken into consideration when preparing necessary plans for this end. Accurate information and data on Hajj statistics is also provided to researchers, academics, beneficiaries, and those interested in the field and is used as well in the evaluation of activities and events held by pilgrim service entities and for learning about trends and patterns of national and foreign pilgrims. GAS will publish the final and official number of Hajj pilgrims on the 9th of Dhu al-Hijjah in the evening through the bulletin of Hajj statistics which will include the following detailed results: Number of national pilgrims, number of foreign incoming pilgrims, number of pilgrims by gender, nationality, and country of departure, numbers and models of vehicles transporting pilgrims, and other detailed data.
Number of passengers through land ports exceeds 70.7 million in 2025
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) released the Road Transport Statistics 2025 publication, which showed that the total number of arriving and departing passengers through land ports exceeded 70.7 million, representing an increase of 7.2% compared to 2024. The number of arriving passengers exceeded 37.0 million, while the number of departing passengers reached approximately 33.7 million, accounting for 47.7% of total passenger traffic. King Fahd Causeway Port accounted for 49.1% of total passenger traffic, followed by Al-Khafji Port at 12.2%. January recorded the highest passenger traffic during the year, with over 7.4 million passengers, while March recorded the lowest passenger traffic, at approximately 4.6 million passengers.Regarding road freight, the total quantity of freight exported and imported through land ports exceeded 26.8 million tons, representing an increase of 4.4% compared to 2024. The quantity of exports increased by 6.3%, reaching 14.3 million tons, while the quantity of imports increased by 2.4%, reaching 12.5 million tons. Al-Batha Port ranked first in freight exports, accounting for 27.9% of total exports, and in freight imports, accounting for 31.9% of total imports, followed by Al-Hadithah Port in freight exports at 23.2% and Riyadh Dry Port in freight imports at 26.0%.The results showed that the number of registered and roadworthy vehicles exceeded 16.7 million by the end of 2025, representing an increase of 5.3% compared to the end of 2024. Private vehicles accounted for 71.2% of the total, followed by private transportation vehicles at 20.0% and public transportation vehicles at 4.4%. The number of newly registered vehicles during the year reached approximately 1.1 million, representing an increase of 3.6%.Regarding driving licenses, the total number of valid driving licenses by the end of 2025 exceeded 11.3 million, with private licenses accounting for 87.2% of the total. Saudis accounted for 51.0% of valid driving license holders, compared to 49.0% for non-Saudis. The number of newly issued driving licenses during the year reached approximately 1.2 million, representing a decrease of 8.1% compared to 2024.It is noteworthy that Road Transport Statistics is an annual publication that provides indicators on passenger traffic, freight traffic, road infrastructure, and road traffic. It is based on administrative records from a number of government and private entities, as well as statistical models, and serves as a reference for studies, research, and local, regional, and international comparisons.
GASTAT 99.6% of individuals aged 15-74 use the internet in the Kingdom in 2026
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) released the results of the ICT Access and Usage Statistics by Households and Individuals 2026 survey, which showed that 99.6% of individuals aged 15–74 use the Internet. The percentage reached 99.8% among Saudi males, compared to 99.5% among Saudi females. The 15–24 age group recorded the highest Internet usage percentage among all age groups, at 99.9% of its total population.Results also indicated that 99.3% of individuals use the Internet at home, while 98.0% use it at least once a day. Regarding the devices used to access the Internet, mobile phone usage reached 98.4%, compared to 13.9% for laptops and 12.3% for tablets.Participation in social networking platforms ranked first among Internet usage activities, accounting for 99.7% of all Internet users, with equal percentages among males and females. This is followed by seeking information about goods and services, at 65.4%. The results also showed that 87.8% of Internet users watch video content online, while 55.2% listen to audio content. Meanwhile, 19.4% of Internet users use the Internet to play games.Regarding e-government services, the percentage of individuals accessing government services online reached 98.4%. The percentage among Saudis stood at 98.6%, reaching 98.9% among males, compared to 98.4% among females. Accessing electronic government forms ranked first among the services used, accounting for 89.0% of all individuals accessing government services online.In terms of e-commerce, 74.5% of all Internet users purchase goods and services online. The percentage among Saudis reached 84.9%, at 86.8% among Saudi females, compared to 83.1% among Saudi males. Clothing, footwear, sports goods, household goods, and other items topped the list of online purchases, accounting for 85.2%, followed by food, groceries, or tobacco at 54.4%, while purchases of books, magazines, and newspapers accounted for 22.8%.It is worth noting that GASTAT produces its statistics in accordance with a unified methodology aligned with procedures adopted by international organizations. This enables international comparability of indicators and meets the requirements of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) indicators.
GASTAT: Operating revenues of the business sector grow by 2.3% in 2025
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) released the Structural Business Statistics 2025 results, which showed that the business sector's operating revenues increased by 2.3% compared to the previous year, reaching SAR 5,628 billion.Results also showed that compensation of employees increased by 6.0%, reaching SAR 624.1 billion. Of this total, SAR 528.9 billion was allocated to salaries and wages, accounting for 84.8% of total compensation, while SAR 95.1 billion was allocated to benefits and allowances, representing 15.2%. Total gross fixed capital formation reached SAR 992 billion, recording a growth of 0.4%, while the gross operating surplus amounted to SAR 2,542 billion, decreasing by 0.1%.It is worth noting that Structural Business Statistics provide detailed indicators on the structure of economic activities in the Kingdom, covering operating revenues and expenditures, as well as compensation of employees. The data are classified according to the International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC4).
GASTAT Updates the Methodology for Calculating WPI
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) updated the methodology for calculating the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) in Saudi Arabia as part of its efforts to develop price statistics, keep pace with economic developments and changes, and apply relevant international statistical practices and standards.The updated methodology includes several improvements, including updating the reference year and relative weights, enhancing geographical coverage, updating and expanding the basket of goods, increasing the number of pricing points and price quotations, and updating the classification used. These improvements enhance the comprehensiveness of the index and its representativeness of wholesale markets in Saudi Arabia.GASTAT has adopted 2023 as the new reference year for the index, replacing the previous reference year of 2014. The relative weights of the index items have also been revised based on the 2023 Supply and Use Tables, import values, and agricultural census data. Furthermore, an annual chain-linking methodology has been introduced, enabling the relative weights to be updated each year to reflect the relative importance of domestic and imported goods included in the index basket and considering developments in the structure of goods traded in wholesale markets.The updated methodology also expands the basket of goods from 343 to 554 items and introduces a revised product classification system. In addition, the number of cities covered by the index has increased from 3 to 23, ensuring coverage of all 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia. These changes were accompanied by updates to the sample design and the sampling frame of pricing points, as well as the use of geospatial technologies and data to distribute the covered pricing points.It is worth noting that the WPI is a periodic statistical product among the economic statistics published monthly by GASTAT. The results will be published tomorrow, Thursday, in accordance with the updated methodology.
GASTAT 59.9% of adults in Saudi Arabia engage in physical activity for 150 minutes or more per week
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) released its Physical Activity Statistics Publication for 2026, which showed that 59.9% of Saudi Arabia's adult population (aged 18 years and above) engage in physical activity for 150 minutes or more per week. The percentage reached 63.4% among males, compared to 52.9% among females. The age group (18–29 years) ranked first at 63.0%, while individuals aged 80 years and above recorded the lowest percentage at 13.4%.In terms of physical activity types, recreational physical activity ranked first among physically active individuals at 64.7%, followed by physical activity while commuting to and from places at 52.4%, and physical activity at work at 40.8%. The average daily time adults spent sitting or reclining, excluding sleep, was 340 minutes, with 363 minutes among females compared to 329 minutes among males.Regarding children and adolescents (aged 5–17 years), the results showed that 20.3% engage in physical activity for 60 minutes or more per day, with the percentage reaching 27.1% among males, compared to 13.3% among females. The results also showed that 33.8% of children and adolescents spend one hour to less than two hours per day sitting or engaging in sedentary activities, such as watching television or using electronic devices, excluding time spent at school.It is noteworthy that the 2026 Physical Activity Statistics Publication presents physical activity indicators for adults (aged 18 years and above) and children and adolescents (aged 5–17 years) in Saudi Arabia, based on the results of the National Health Survey and the Woman and Child Health Survey. Its estimates are based on self-reported data provided by individuals or caregivers.

The product provides detailed data and indicators covering exports, re-exports, and imports in accordance with approved statistical classifications.The product aims to improve the availability of up-to-date non-oil international trade data, supporting the monitoring and analysis of developments and enabling users to benefit from relevant indicators in a timely manner. GASTAT: Exports and imports at Saudi Arabia’s ports exceed 341 million tons in 2025
The General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) released the Maritime Transport Statistics publication 2025, which indicated that the total volume of inbound and outbound cargo at Saudi Arabia’s ports exceeded 341 million tons. The volume of outbound cargo reached 227.2 million tons, increasing by 2.1% compared to 2024, while the volume of inbound cargo totaled 114.3 million tons, an increase of 5.0% compared to the previous year.The publication indicated that King Fahad Industrial Port in Yanbu ranked first in terms of exports, representing 50.7% of the total, with 115.2 million tons. Meanwhile, King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam ranked first among ports receiving imported goods, with a total of 39.2 million tons.Data also showed that the total volume of cargo handled at Saudi Arabia’s ports exceeded 298 million tons in 2025. King Fahad Industrial Port in Yanbu ranked first in terms of cargo handling, accounting for 33.0%, while Jeddah Islamic Port ranked first in terms of handling twenty-foot equivalent units, accounting for 47.3%.Regarding ship traffic, the total number of ships arriving at Saudi Arabia’s ports reached 7,848 in 2025. Jeddah Islamic Port accounted for the largest share, with 2,677 ships, followed by Jubail Commercial Port with 1,256 ships and King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam with 1,030 ships.The publication also showed that the number of passengers arriving at and departing from Saudi Arabia’s ports reached approximately 1.1 million passengers in 2025, an increase of 20.1% compared to 2024. Jazan Port recorded the highest passenger traffic, with more than 489.6 thousand passengers, followed by Jeddah Islamic Port with approximately 413.8 thousand passengers and NEOM Port with more than 187 thousand passengers. Meanwhile, the total number of cruise passengers reached 174.5 thousand during the year.
It is worth noting that the Maritime Transport Statistics publication is based on administrative records obtained from the Saudi Ports Authority, the Economic Cities and Special Economic Zones Authority, NEOM Company, and the Royal Commission for Jubail and Yanbu, with the aim of providing a reliable database to support studies and research in the maritime transport sector.
