### /props/pageProps/newsDetails/content
Makkah, July 11, 2023, SPA -- The Saudi Riwaq (Portico) of the Grand Mosque, with an area of 210,000 square meters, will contribute to boosting the capacity of the areas for worship.
    The four-floor Riwaq accommodates up to 287,000 worshippers and allows for 107,000 tawaf (circumambulation around Kaabah) performers per hour.
    The Riwaq was extended from the west with King Fahd bin Abdulaziz ordering its expansion, which included around 1,500 white marble-covered columns and a number of domes over the porticos.
    In the era of King Fahd bin Abdulaziz, the Riwaq expanded the Grand Mosque’s area to around 365,000 square meters to accommodate about a million worshippers.
    After that extension, the Saudi Riwaq included a new gate named after King Fahd.
    On the northern side, the Riwaq also witnessed a new expansion that began during the reign of King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz and was completed during the reign of King Salman bin Abdulaziz.
    This expansion effort, which included many columns and a door named after King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz, has increased the Grand Mosque’s area to about 1 million square meters with a capacity of around 2 million worshippers.
    --SPA
