The '''King Abdulaziz Complex for Holy Kaaba Kiswa''' ({{Langx|ar|مجمع الملك عبدالعزيز لكسوة الكعبة المشرفة|rtl=yes}}) is a specialized facility in [[Mecca]], [[Saudi Arabia]], responsible for producing the ''[[kiswah]]'', the black silk covering adorned with [[Quran]] [[Āyah|verses]] that envelops the [[Kaaba]] in the [[Masjid al-Haram]]. The complex oversees the design, manufacturing, and annual replacement of the ''kiswah'', which comprise a significant religious and cultural tradition in [[Islam]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2020-07-23 |title=The Kiswa: The story behind the covering of the holy Kaaba |url=https://arab.news/r6ps2 |access-date=2025-09-22 |website=Arab News |language=en}}</ref>

== History ==
The tradition of covering the [[Kaaba]] dates back to the [[Pre-Islamic Arabia|pre-Islamic era]]. During the reign of [[Al-Ma'mun|Caliph al-Ma'mun]], the [[Kiswah]] was replaced three times a year. The practice of inscribing on the [[Kiswah]] began during the [[Abbasid Caliphate|Abbasid era]], with [[Caliphate|caliphs]] recording their names and the place and date of manufacture.<ref name=":3">{{Cite journal |last=Mortel |first=Richard |date=1988 |title=The Kiswa: Its Origins and Development from Pre-Islamic Times until the End of the Mamluk Period |journal=Al-'Usûr |volume=3 |issue=2 |pages=30–46}}</ref><ref name=":4">{{Cite book |last=Nassar |first=Nahla |url=http://archive.org/details/TheHajj |title=The Hajj. Collected Essays |publisher=The British Museum |year=2013 |isbn=9780861591930 |editor=Porter, Venetia |location=London |pages=175–183 |chapter=Dar al-Kiswa al-Sharifa: Administration and Production |editor2=Seif, Liana}}</ref>

In 1927, [[Ibn Saud|King Abdulaziz Al Saud]] established the first [[Kiswah]] workshop in [[Mecca]], marking the beginning of domestic production. This initiative aimed to reduce reliance on imports and ensure the quality and authenticity of the covering.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-06-25 |title=Saudi Arabia's expertise in kiswa manufacturing showcased to guests of Hajj program |url=https://arab.news/68egf |access-date=2025-09-22 |website=Arab News |language=en}}</ref>

The facility, formerly known as the ''Kiswa Factory of the Holy Kaaba'', was renamed in 2018 by [[Decree|royal decree]] issued by [[Salman of Saudi Arabia|King Salman]] to the ''King Abdulaziz Complex for the Holy Kaaba Kiswa'' in honour of the founding king.<ref name=":0" />

== Location and organisation ==
Situated in [[Mecca]], the complex operates under the [[General Presidency of Haramain]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Holy Kaaba Receives New Kiswa for the Year 1447 AH, Weighing Over 1.4 Tons |url=https://spa.gov.sa/ |access-date=2025-09-22 |website=spa.gov.sa |language=en}}</ref> It encompasses various departments, including dyeing, weaving (both manual and machine), printing, [[embroidery]], [[gilding]], [[sewing]], and quality control.

In addition to manufacturing the [[Kiswah]] of [[Kaaba]] and its components such as the [[Kiswah#Hizam|hizam]] ''(belt)'' and [[Kiswah#Sitara|sitara]] ''(door curtain)'', the complex also produces the inner green coloured Kiswa of the [[Kaaba]] and the covering for the Prophet's burial chamber.<ref>{{Cite news |last=عبدالرحمن حذيفة - مكة المكرمة |title=كسوة الكعبة تنتسب للمؤسس |url=http://makkahnewspaper.com/article/602250/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%AF/%D9%83%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%A8-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191213025022/http://makkahnewspaper.com/article/602250/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%AF/%D9%83%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%A8-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3 |archive-date=2019-12-13 |access-date=2025-09-22 |work=Makkah |language=ar-GB}}</ref>

== Materials and manufacture ==
[[File:Kiswah, Kaaba - 7 May 2016 (cropped-01).jpg|thumb|300x300px|[[Quran]] verses embroidered on [[Kiswah]] with gold-plated silver threads]]
The [[Kiswah]] is crafted from black-dyed natural silk and embroidered with gold and silver-plated threads. The process involves several stages:

# '''Dyeing''': Silk threads are dyed using desalinated water to prevent damage to the fabric.
# '''Weaving''': The dyed silk is woven into large panels.
# '''Printing''': [[Quran]] verses are printed onto the fabric.
# '''Embroidery''': Skilled artisans embroider the printed verses using gold and silver-plated threads.
# '''Assembly''': The embroidered panels are sewn together to form the complete Kiswah.

Each year, approximately 825 kilograms of [[silk]], 410 kilograms of raw cotton, 120 kilograms of gold-plated silver thread, and 60 kilograms of pure silver are used in the production.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-25 |title=Replacement of the kiswa that covers Holy Kaaba at Grand Mosque in Makkah begins |url=https://arab.news/9axq3 |access-date=2025-09-22 |website=Arab News |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":0" />

== Annual replacement ceremony ==
The [[Kiswah]] is currently replaced annually on the 1st of [[Muharram]], the first day of the [[Islamic calendar|Islamic lunar calendar]]. Historically, the replacement was performed on the [[Day of Arafah]], before the shift to the current schedule. The ceremony involves the removal of the old Kiswah and the installation of the new covering, a process carried out by a team of trained craftsmen.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Raza |first=Safeer |date=2025-06-26 |title=Saudi Arabia begins annual Kaaba Kiswa replacement ceremony in Makkah |url=https://www.muslimnetwork.tv/saudi-arabia-begins-annual-kaaba-kiswa-replacement-ceremony-in-makkah/ |access-date=2025-09-22 |website=Muslim Network TV |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Participation of women ===
In 2024, for the first time in recorded history, women participated in the ceremonial replacement of the [[Kiswah]]. Female employees of the [[General Presidency of Haramain|General Presidency for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques]] assisted in manufacturing and also by carrying sections of the new [[Kiswah]] and handing them to male colleagues, who then transported the pieces to the [[Kaaba]] in [[Mecca]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Women participate in replacing Kaaba's kiswa for first time in history |url=https://en.royanews.tv/news/52659 |website=Roya News}}</ref>
