## Depiction rules
- **Not depicted in any form:** Ibrahim, Musa, Sulaiman and the messengers in Yasin. For the messengers, show three lights or lanterns.
- **Not visualised:** jinn ('Ifrit), angels and Allah. Do not visualise the man who "had knowledge of the Book" as a figure either. Show the throne arriving through light instead.
- **Faceless, small, reverent silhouettes only:** Musa's mother, Musa's sister, Firaun's wife, the Queen of Saba, the townspeople and the man from the edge of the city.
- **The baby Musa:** never shown. Show only the closed chest.
- **The birds in EP31:** no gore. Show feathers scattered across four hills that fly back and gather into whole birds.
- **No violence:** the death of the man in EP34 is not shown. His lamp goes out and a gate of light opens.
