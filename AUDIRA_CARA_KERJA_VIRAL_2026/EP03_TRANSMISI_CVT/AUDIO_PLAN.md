# EP 03: Matic Tanpa Pindah Gigi: Cara Kerja CVT, YECVT, dan D-CVT

AUDIRA, seri CARA KERJA VIRAL. Created by Agus Dwi R. Status: final dengan voice-over, 6 November 2026.

EP03 sound: a scooter engine whose pitch follows the ratio programme, belt whir, mechanical clicks for each part, electronic blips for the YECVT chapter. Run from this folder.

Musik dan SFX disintesis di `mix.py` (numpy) plus pustaka `AUDIO/SFX/`. Setelah suara dibuat: musik ducking otomatis di bawah vokal, master -14 LUFS, true peak di bawah -1 dBTP.
